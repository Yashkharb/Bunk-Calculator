Built for

Bunk Simulator is a decision instrument, not a percentage printer. We answer one question with precision: Can I miss tomorrow and stay above the required attendance?

Why we built it

Calculators tell you
84.09%. We tell you
what to do.

Most sites compute A / H x 100 and stop. You still have to guess how many bunks you have left, what happens if you miss Monday and Wednesday, or how many straight attends recover you. We built the simulator that should have existed: current - future - decision, live and integer-safe.

No login
Runs in your browser. No account, no tracking.
No server
All math is local. Never leaves the device.
Exact math
Integer-safe with epsilon correction.
Shareable
URL encodes your state.
