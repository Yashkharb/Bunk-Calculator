How it works

The math is simple. The consequences matter.

Everything runs locally in your browser. No data leaves your device. Formulas are integer-safe with epsilon correction to avoid floating-point drift.

1. Current attendance

A ÷ H × 100 — attended over held as a percentage.

37 ÷ 44 × 100 = 84.09%

2. Max classes you can miss

Largest m where A/(H+m) ≥ R. If you’re already below required, this is 0 and we switch to recovery.

37/(44+5)=75.51% ✓  ·  37/(44+6)=74.00% ✗ → buffer 5

3. Consecutive attendances to recover

Smallest x where (A+x)/(H+x) ≥ R. Solved exactly then adjusted for float.

30/44=68.18% → need 12 attends → 42/56=75.00%

4. Future simulation

For a future attends and m misses: (A+a)/(H+a+m). Every toggle recomputes instantly.

Why one miss can hurt

With small H, one bunk swings % a lot. At 37/44 (84%), missing 1 drops to 82.22% (−1.87 pts). At 80/100, missing 1 is −0.79 pts. The simulator makes this visible.

Quick mental formula
Required held for R%: ceil(A×100÷R)
Remaining buffer ≈ ceil(A×100÷R) − H

Capped at 0 if already below. For 75% we avoid float drift with ×100 integer check.

FAQ

Student questions, answered

How is attendance percentage calculated?

Divide classes attended by classes held and multiply by 100: A ÷ H × 100. Example: 37 attended out of 44 held = 37 ÷ 44 × 100 = 84.09%.

How many classes can I miss and stay above 75%?

We find the largest number of consecutive misses m where A/(H+m) ≥ 75%. For 37/44, that’s 5 misses (37/49 = 75.51%). The 6th miss drops to 74%. Your buffer updates live as you type.

What if I’m already below 75% — how many must I attend?

We solve for the smallest consecutive attends x where (A+x)/(H+x) ≥ R. Example: 30/44 at 68.18% needs 12 straight attends to hit 75% (42/56).

What is “attendance buffer”?

Your buffer is how many classes you can miss before falling below required %. It’s your breathing room: Safe (≥4), Getting close (2–3), Critical (1), Below (0 + recovery). We never use color alone — every state has an icon and label.

How do I calculate attendance manually?

Use A/H×100 then compare to your required %. To check “can I miss one?”, compute A/(H+1). The simulator does this for 1,2,3,5,10 misses automatically.

Why does one bunk drop so much sometimes?

Because percentage is A/H, the effect of one miss is larger when H is small. Early in the semester, bunks are expensive. Later, they dilute. That’s why the week planner helps — see the whole week before you decide.

Is my data saved or sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally. URL sharing is optional and encodes numbers in the address bar — no backend, no login, no tracking beyond privacy-friendly minimal analytics.
