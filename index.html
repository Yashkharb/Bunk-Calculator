03 — Method

The math is simple.
The consequences matter.

Everything runs locally. Formulas are integer-safe with epsilon correction.

1. Current attendance

A ÷ H × 100

37 ÷ 44 × 100 = 84.09%

2. Max classes you can miss

Largest m with A/(H+m) ≥ R

37/(44+5)=75.51% ✓ · 37/(44+6)=74.00% ✗

3. Recovery

Smallest x with (A+x)/(H+x) ≥ R

30/44=68.18% → need 12 → 42/56=75.00%

4. Future simulation

(A+a)/(H+a+m) — every toggle recomputes.

Why one miss can hurt

With small H, one bunk swings a lot. At 37/44, missing 1 drops 1.87 pts. At 80/100, just 0.79.

Quick mental formula
Required held for R%: ceil(A×100÷R)
Remaining buffer ≈ ceil(A×100÷R) − H

04 — FAQ

Student questions,
answered

How is attendance percentage calculated? —

Divide attended by held and multiply by 100: A ÷ H × 100. Example: 37 ÷ 44 × 100 = 84.09%.

How many classes can I miss and stay above 75%? —

Largest m with A/(H+m) ≥ 75%. For 37/44, that's 5 misses (37/49 = 75.51%).

What if I'm already below 75% — how many must I attend? —

Smallest x with (A+x)/(H+x) ≥ R. Example: 30/44 needs 12 straight attends to reach 75%.

What is “attendance buffer”? —

How many classes you can miss before falling below required %. Safe (≥4), Close (2–3), Critical (1), Below (recovery).

Is my data saved or sent anywhere? —

No. Everything runs locally. URL sharing is optional and encodes numbers in the address bar.
